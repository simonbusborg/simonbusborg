### I work where design, code and AI meet, and right now most of my curiosity goes into what AI does to the way we make things.

I founded WEAREHEAVY in 2016 and run it as managing partner and board member, while still working hands-on as a creative technologist. We're an AI-first studio and part of Dwarf. We design, develop, explore and innovate with AI for brands, cultural institutions and agencies, from Krigsmuseet, Kronborg and Nationalmuseet to Velux, Rockwool, Ørsted, Google, Roskilde Festival and American Express.

Outside client work I explore by making small tools for my own life. These days I use more apps on my phone that I made myself than apps made by others, all running on the same design framework. One of them, Polaris, an open-source menu bar app for Polestar owners, has passed 500 downloads. Those experiments feed straight back into the work we do for clients.

Before WEAREHEAVY I was a frontend developer at Umbraco, working on the backoffice for versions 7 and 8 and on Umbraco Cloud. Along the way I started a network for designers and developers, organised an international conference, and saw a weekend project become a top Product Hunt launch in 2016.

I've been on the Awwwards jury since 2018 and served on the CSS Design Awards jury before that.
